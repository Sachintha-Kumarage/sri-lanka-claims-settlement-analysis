# Claims Settlement Performance in the Sri Lankan General Insurance Industry (2022-2025)

Analysis of how 14 general insurers and the main insurance categories settled claims, using statistics published by the Insurance Regulatory Commission of Sri Lanka (IRCSL).

![Industry claims settlement ratio](charts/industry_trend.png)

## Key findings

| Year | Settled by number | Settled by value |
|---|---|---|
| 2022 | 75.2% | 59.9% |
| 2023 | 79.6% | 61.3% |
| 2024 | 77.4% | 57.5% |
| 2025 | 78.3% | 43.0% |

- Fire insurance made up 50.8% of unpaid claim value in 2025.
- IRCSL links the low 2025 value ratios mainly to Cyclone Ditwah claims still pending at year-end.
- Excluding crop insurance, the ratio by number fell from 77.4% in 2024 to 75.0% in 2025.

## Method

Claims settlement ratio = claims paid / claims received x 100, by number and by value. Claims received include opening outstanding, newly reported and reopened claims.

## Limitations

- Only four years of data; settlement time is not measured.
- Company claim counts are not published in the reports used, so company averages are unweighted.
- Category definitions change between years.
- Rejection ratios by insurance category are not published in the reports used.
